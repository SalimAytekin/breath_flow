# BreathFlow Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** February 17, 2026  
**Contact:** dxdiag.app@gmail.com

## 1. Data We Collect
- Account data: UID, email, display name, profile photo, account creation date, last login date.
- Premium status: subscription type (monthly/annual), `isPremium`, `premiumExpiryDate`.
- Activity metrics: total meditation minutes, total sessions, streak days, last meditation date, completed journeys.
- Favorites & preferences: favorite breathing exercises/sounds, daily goal, reminder time, preferred theme, notification status.
- Sleep journal data: date, bedtime, wake time, mood selection, dream notes, sleep quality notes.
- Additional subcollections: activities, stats, premium records.
- Analytics & ads: Firebase Analytics events, Crashlytics crash logs, AdMob ad impressions.
- Local-only data: sleep entries stored in SharedPreferences before sign-in.

## 2. How We Use Data
- Personalize the app (favorites, goals, reminders, theming).
- Display and sync progress analytics.
- Validate premium access and entitlements.
- Troubleshoot crashes and improve performance.
- Serve ads via Google AdMob.

## 3. Data Storage
- Cloud data is stored in Firebase Firestore under Google Cloud security standards.
- Local data on the device is removed automatically when you uninstall the app.
- Syncing occurs only while you are signed in.

## 4. Third-Party Services
- Firebase Auth, Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config.
- Google AdMob (subject to Google’s personalized ads policy; preferences configurable in Google Ads settings).
- Google Play Billing for Monthly/Annual Premium subscriptions; cancellations/refunds follow Google Play rules.

## 5. Health Warning
- BreathFlow does not provide medical advice or treatment.
- Consult your doctor if you have heart/vascular disease, hypertension, asthma/COPD, neurological disorders, panic disorder, pregnancy, or other chronic conditions.
- Stop immediately and seek medical help if you feel dizziness, chest pain, numbness, or shortness of breath during exercises.
- Do not use the app while driving or performing activities that require full attention.

## 6. Data Deletion
- Local data is deleted when you uninstall the app.
- To delete your account and Firestore records, email dxdiag.app@gmail.com from your registered address. Requests are completed within 30 days.

## 7. Your Rights (GDPR / KVKK)
- You may request access, rectification, deletion, restriction, or objection to the processing of your data via dxdiag.app@gmail.com.

## 8. Children’s Privacy
- BreathFlow is for users aged 13+. If you become aware of data collected from a child under 13, contact us and we will remove it promptly.

## 9. Changes
- We may update this policy and will revise the “Last updated” date. Significant changes may be announced in-app.

## 10. Summary
- Your data stays secure on your device and in Firestore.
- We do not sell personal data to third parties.
- Ads are managed by Google AdMob.
- Questions? Contact dxdiag.app@gmail.com.
